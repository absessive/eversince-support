Privacy Policy

This privacy policy is for EverSince, an iOS app for tracking moments, days until, and streaks you want to remember.

Effective date: May 15, 2026

Summary

EverSince is designed to be private and local-first. The app does not include analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs, third-party tracking, a custom backend, or a separate EverSince account system. Reminder notifications, if enabled, are scheduled on your device.

Information You Add

You may add moments, dates, directions, streaks, categories, icons, colors, and reminder preferences. This information is stored locally on your iPhone in the current release.

No Account Login

EverSince does not ask you to create a separate app account, and it does not receive or store your Apple ID password.

No Analytics, Ads, or Third-Party SDKs

EverSince does not use analytics tools, advertising networks, third-party tracking SDKs, or external login services. The app does not sell personal data.

No Custom Backend Account

EverSince does not operate a custom backend login system for the iOS app. There is no EverSince email/password account to create, manage, or reset.

Offline Use

EverSince can be used offline. Changes made while offline are stored locally on your iPhone.

Reminders

If you enable streak reminders, EverSince asks iOS for notification permission and schedules local reminder notifications on your device. EverSince does not send reminder data to a custom server.

Support Requests

If you contact support, you may choose to include information such as your email address, device model, iOS version, EverSince version, and a description of the issue. Support messages are used only to respond to your request.

Children's Privacy

EverSince is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as EverSince changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact: contact@absessive.com.
